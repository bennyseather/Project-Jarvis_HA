# M36 - Contextual Jarvis Personality

## Outcome

Jarvis applies one inspectable British-English personality profile after
reasoning, orchestration and policy decisions. Formality, warmth, verbosity,
restrained humour, proactivity and preferred address are configurable from the
Home Assistant app and through explicit conversation controls.

## Context and diagnostics

Presentation recognises protected exact responses, research summaries, home
interactions, informational dialogue and general dialogue. `show personality
diagnostics` reports the profile, mode and bounded influences used for the last
reply without retaining hidden psychological traits or exposing prompt content.

Identity questions use explicit local memory and approved household knowledge
first. Public research may supplement the answer only when the subject is
sufficiently disambiguated. Jarvis never merges people or infers sensitive
traits.

## Boundaries

Personality cannot change facts, structured results, entity selection,
permissions, risk, confirmations or execution. Safety, failure, emergency and
confirmation responses remain exact and humour-free. Voice-model training,
actor imitation, additional Home Assistant authority and workload migration
remain outside this milestone.
