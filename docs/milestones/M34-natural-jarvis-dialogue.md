# M34 - Natural Jarvis Dialogue

## Outcome

Jarvis now treats bounded Home Assistant conversation history as a continuous
dialogue. Clear follow-ups and corrections retain their preceding subject while
ambiguous references still produce a concise clarification.

## Presentation

- British-English personality guidance applies consistently to conversation,
  research, home status, actions, confirmations, and failures.
- Voice responses are shorter, remove redundant acknowledgement openings, and
  use speech-friendly punctuation.
- Researched voice answers never speak source links. Structured sources remain
  attached to the result and text-mode answers retain their source list.
- Preferred forms of address remain explicit and are used only when socially
  natural.

## Boundaries

Personality changes presentation only. It cannot change facts, entity scope,
permissions, confirmation requirements, risk handling, or action execution.
M34 adds no emotion simulation, automatic profiling, background microphone,
voice-model replacement, or new Home Assistant authority.
